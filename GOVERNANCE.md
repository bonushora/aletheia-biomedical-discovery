# Governance

This foundation consolidates approved platform principles without replacing the operational BH-SEP, BH-SDP, or containment protocols.

- Evidence precedes scientific claims.
- Human scientific authority is the final decision boundary.
- Observation, interpretation, and intervention are distinct states.
- Scientific data and state must never be mutated silently.
- A generated hypothesis is not a validated finding.
- Provenance and reproducibility accompany every qualified artifact.
- Scientific state transitions are explicit and auditable.
- The platform is research-only and has no independent clinical authority.
- AI models and agents have no independent scientific authority.
- Repository federation is explicit: cross-repository mutation requires a separately authorized mission.

ADR status remains authoritative. Except for accepted ADR-0016, the foundation ADRs remain proposals pending explicit human decisions.
