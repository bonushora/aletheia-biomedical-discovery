# ADR-KR-0001 — Kidney Regeneration Research Control Plane Scope

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Parent:** ADR-0002

## Decision
KRRCP is the first disease/organ-specific Research Control Plane in Aletheia. Its scope includes kidney developmental biology, kidney organoids, single-cell/multi-omic evidence, injury/repair states, fibrosis, regenerative mechanisms, cross-dataset replication, candidate generation, and experimental handoff.

It is not a clinical nephrology decision-support system and does not claim whole-organ regeneration or therapeutic efficacy without appropriate evidence.
